## 0.2.1 — 2026-07-29

> **Upgrade note — you stay signed in longer, but you sign in once more now.**
> A phone used regularly is no longer logged out 12 hours after pairing: the
> window now renews with activity, under a 7 day ceiling from the original
> sign-in. Upgrading restarts the app, which signs every device out as it
> always has, so scan one fresh QR and the new behaviour applies from there.

### Added

- **auth** — Keep an active phone signed in, cap the session at 7 days (#2)

### Documentation

- Require PRs into main and consent before a version bump (#1)



## 0.2.0 — 2026-07-29

> **Upgrade note — password sign-in is now off by default.**
> A username/password that a whole LAN, or a public tunnel, can attempt is the
> weakest door this app has, so a fresh install no longer opens it. Existing
> installs that never set the toggle lose password sign-in at next launch and
> the startup log says so. Pair with a QR, or turn it back on in
> **Settings → Access**.
